Squat-jump performance dashboard

Twenty squat jumps by the same participant, filmed by the Raspberry Pi camera. Take-off and landing frames were annotated by hand; flight time gives jump height, force and power through the ballistic model used on the Pi (mechanics.py). The lower panels show how the automatic detector performs on videos it was not trained on.

Method and assumptions

Model-derived estimates, not force-plate measurements.

T = tlanding − ttake-off  (flight time)
h = g·T² / 8  (jump height)
v0 = g·T / 2  (take-off velocity of the centre of mass)
F̄ = m·g·(1 + h / d)  (mean vertical force during push-off)
v̄ = v0 / 2  ;  P̄ = F̄·v̄  (mean push-off power)
