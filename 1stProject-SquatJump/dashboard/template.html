Jump performance dashboard

Twenty CMJs by Joël, hands on hips, according to Victor’s 21 October account; recorded with the Raspberry Pi camera. Take-off and landing frames were annotated by hand; flight time estimates height; force and power additionally depend on unverified push-off assumptions in the model used on the Pi (mechanics.py). The lower panels show how the automatic detector performs on videos it was not trained on.

Participant inputs

Same inputs as the Pi program. Changing them recomputes force and power instantly.

Training dataset · 20 trials

Method and assumptions
Model-derived estimates, not force-plate measurements.

    

      

        T = tlanding − ttake-off  (flight time)

        h = g·T² / 8  (jump height)

        v0 = g·T / 2  (take-off velocity of the centre of mass)

        F̄ = m·g·(1 + h / d)  (mean vertical force during push-off)

        v̄ = v0 / 2  ;  P̄ = F̄·v̄  (mean push-off power)
